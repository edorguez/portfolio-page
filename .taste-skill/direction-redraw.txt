This is the direction for this build. Adopt it. Do not blend it with the layout you would have made anyway,
and do not redraw because it is unfamiliar: unfamiliar is the point. Redraw a slot only when it conflicts
with a stated brand constraint or needs an asset that does not exist, and say why in your report.

The parts of this sheet were written separately and will disagree in small ways. Settle every disagreement in this order:
**house rules** (including type no smaller than 13px and body text of 16px or more) → **the direction** (the idea, the composition habits, where the
one accent goes) → **the type system and the palette** (which face does which job, the five size steps, which colour is the ground and where the
accent goes) → **the hero blueprint** → **the section blueprints**. Under each blueprint below, "Adjusted for this page" lists where it disagrees.
In practice: take geometry from blueprints (columns, proportions, what sits where) and take everything else from above them. Map every size a
blueprint names onto one of your five type steps instead of adding a sixth. Use one button shape, one corner radius and one pair of page margins on
the whole page, whatever individual blueprints say. If a blueprint names a kind of typeface (a light serif, a heavy grotesk), use the dealt face.

## 1. The idea
The first screen is made only of words, colour and space, good enough that nobody misses a picture there. Pictures arrive later, large and few, as relief from the type. This is the honest answer when a subject has nothing worth photographing: instead of inventing illustrations, set what you have to say as well as it can be set. The typography carries a point of view through scale (one enormous statement, everything else small), through measure (a narrow, bookish column or a full-width line), and through one committed colour.

Before anything else, write one sentence in your notes: "On this page, <the subject's own material> becomes <the device>."
Fill the first blank from your inventory of what only this client has (its name and what it means, its street, its numbers, its
constraint), then put the nearest competitor in the sentence: if it still works, the idea is the category's, not the client's.
Then name the one live thing the visitor can do on the page (SKILL.md step 4).

## 2. Composition
First screen: the statement, two lines at most, set at 9–14vw and occupying the full width with tight leading, positioned in the upper two-thirds. One supporting sentence at 20–24px sits bottom-left on the same margin and the single action sits bottom-right on the same baseline. No navigation bar; two or three links in a top corner. Sections that follow are typographic set pieces, each different: a list of five to eight capabilities set at 4–6vw one per line; a paragraph at 28–36px in a 28ch column pushed to the right half; a pair of large numerals with a sentence each; a single quotation filling a colour field. Each section uses a different horizontal position (left, right, full) so the page has movement without imagery.

## 3. Type
Kept from the brand. This direction lives or dies on the face and its settings. Display at tight tracking and leading; text sizes few and far apart (for example 15, 22, 34 and the display size). Use real typographic detail: proper quotes, balanced line breaks, hung punctuation where it helps.

Bottle green covers the page and warm gold sets all the type, with panels one step lighter than the ground. The button is solid gold with green text, and photographs are kept dark and warm so the pair is not disturbed.
Either one saturated ground for the whole page with a single ink, or paper and ink with one section inverted and one in the accent as a full field. Colour does the job images would have done.

## 6. Hero — Waterline headline
Two flat fields fill the viewport and meet at exactly half height: the upper one paper or white, the lower one a saturated palette colour. No image. The headline is two lines of capitals in a black-weight grotesk at clamp(72px, 9vw, 140px), centred, leading 0.86, about 65% of the viewport wide, positioned so the seam cuts through the middle of the second line. Above the seam the letters take the accent ink; below it they switch to a deep ink, as if under water. Build it with two stacked copies of the headline in the same position (the lower copy aria-hidden), each clipped to its own field with clip-path: inset(), or with mix-blend-mode: difference on a single copy if the colours allow. The wordmark sits top-left and the single action, a solid rectangle in the accent ink, top-right, both on 28px margins; there are no other links. Near the bottom, 40px above the edge, one supporting sentence at 20px is centred in the paper colour on the saturated field. Everything else is empty colour.

On a phone: The same two fields, meeting at 46% of the height. The headline sets at 15.5vw on two lines, filling the width inside 16px margins, the seam still cutting the second line. The wordmark sits top-left and the action top-right as a small solid rectangle. The supporting sentence sits bottom-left at 17px on the saturated field, on up to three lines.
Headline: A blunt statement of 2-4 words in capitals, at most 20 characters, split so each line is one or two words.

Adjusted for this page:
- It names a kind of typeface: use the brand faces.
- It names colours (white, black): map them onto this palette's roles (ground `--bg`, panels `--surface` and the fields, text `--ink`/`--muted`, the accent `--accent` on the primary action only).
- It blends text with `difference`: over anything but black, white or grey that gives random hues. Use a solid ink chosen for each ground instead.

## 7. Sections, in order
Each is a composition to rebuild with this project's content. Keep the geometry; change the subject. Re-order or drop a
section when the argument of the page needs it (see references/copy.md), never to fall back to a card grid.

### 1. about — Side facts, wide paragraph (table)
Two rows share one vertical line at 34% of the viewport width. Row one: in a 160px column at the left margin, three label and value pairs stack with 20px between them, each a label in the soft ink with its value beneath in full ink, all at 13px (founded, based in, team size). From the 34% line a five-line paragraph at clamp(20px, 1.7vw, 26px), leading 1.35, runs to 90% of the width. Row two starts 120px lower and repeats the logic for the record of the practice: one plain word at 13px at the far left (awards, press, clients), then from the 34% line two list columns, each 260px wide with a 64px gutter, at 13px with 10px between lines: name at the left, year at the right in the soft ink. No heading; the paragraph opens the section. Each list comfortably holds 6–12 lines. No photographs are needed.
On a phone: Everything aligns to the left margin. The paragraph comes first at 19px. The three label and value pairs follow as a two-column grid at 14px. The two record lists stack with 40px between them, each opened by its plain word in full ink.

### 2. stats — Total and its receipt (split)
A total at giant size, and beside it the slip that adds up to it. Left, columns 1–6: at the top the heading, two to five words at 40–44px, with a two-line note at 16px; at the bottom-left, anchored to the section's lower edge, the total at clamp(160px, 18vw, 300px) with leading 0.8, and one caption line at 17px under it. The space between heading and total is left empty. Right, columns 8–11: a paper-coloured slip 380–440px wide on the section's contrasting ground, top-aligned with the heading, its bottom edge a zigzag made with clip-path. The slip lists eight to fourteen rows in the text face at 15–16px with tabular numerals, grouped under bold group names; each row has a description on the left, a right-aligned value, and a leader of round 4px dots between. The last row is inverted on a solid ink bar and repeats the total. The slip's values must truly sum to the figure.
On a phone: Heading and note come first. The total follows at 40vw, flush left, with its caption. The slip comes last at full width minus 16px margins with its zigzag edge, rows at 15px, and the inverted total row closing it.

### 3. work — Book-spine index (index)
The complete index in the footprint of a banner. Twenty to forty project names are set as single lines rotated to read top to bottom with `writing-mode: vertical-rl`, packed side by side like book spines across the full content width. Each spine is a column 30–34px wide and 420px tall: the name at 18px starts at the top, and the discipline follows after a 12px gap on a flat highlighter-colour background chosen from four palette colours by discipline. Spines are separated only by 6px of space. Hovering or focusing a spine turns it to full ink and dims the others to 40%; the whole spine is the link. A two- or three-word heading at 28px sits top-left above the shelf with a one-sentence note at 15px to its right on the same baseline. No images. Below the shelf, 96px of empty ground. It turns a long client list into one dense typographic object.
On a phone: Rotation is dropped. Names become a horizontal wrap: each name at 17px followed by its highlighted discipline, items flowing inline with 10px gaps and 14px of line spacing, so the index reads as one dense paragraph of links filling the width.

Adjusted for this page:
- It fades text: anything a visitor reads, inactive items of a list included, is `--ink` or `--muted` at full strength (contrast 4.5:1 or more). The active item is told apart by `--ink` against `--muted`, weight or the accent, not by fading the rest.

### 4. showcase — Two giant words compared (statement)
No photographs: the thing shown is a pair of words, characters or figures that make the point by comparison (before and after, input and output, ours and the usual). At the top of the section, on one row: the heading, two to four words at 28–36px, at the left margin, and a paragraph of up to 60 words at 17px starting at column 7. Then 12vh of space. Below, two specimens side by side, one starting on column 1 and one on column 7, each set at clamp(180px, 34vw, 520px) with leading 0.8, so each fills about half the viewport's width. The section has a fixed height and overflow: hidden, so its bottom edge cuts both specimens at roughly 60% of their height. One short caption at 16px sits above the top-left of each specimen, naming it. Both specimens share one ink; the difference is in the letterforms or values, not in colour.
On a phone: Heading first, then the paragraph, at full width. The two specimens stack, each at 62vw type size in its own 38svh box with overflow hidden so the lower part is cut. Captions sit above each box at 15px. The second box takes the inverse colours of the first.

### 5. list — Line-up at one type size (table)
A full-bleed flat section. The programme is a four-column table spanning the full width between 24px margins, with every cell at one size, clamp(20px, 2.1vw, 30px), one weight, no bold: the act or session flush left, the format starting at 33%, the room or stage at 66%, and the time range right-aligned at the far edge with tabular figures. Rows are 1.5em tall and separated by nothing but that leading; the fixed column starts do the organising. Day or part headings are a single line at the same size in the accent ink with 72px above and 20px below, and may be `position: sticky; top: 0` on a strip of the section's ground colour so the current day stays in view. Breaks and changeovers are rows set in capitals in the soft ink. There is no other heading. Comfortably holds 12–40 rows; with fewer than eight, raise the size to 3vw.
On a phone: Each entry becomes two lines: the act at 22px on the first, then format, room and time on one line at 14px in the soft ink with the time right-aligned. 18px between entries. Day headings stay sticky at the top on the ground colour.

Adjusted for this page:
- It names a kind of typeface: use the brand faces.

### 6. contact — Address in display rows (list)
The contact block is a stack of five full-width rows, every one set at the same size, clamp(44px, 6vw, 88px), regular weight, left-aligned with a 20px inset and leading 1.0: the building or venue name, the street, the city with postcode, the opening hours as one compact line, the phone number. Each row is about 150px tall with the text sitting in its lower third; rows are told apart by alternating two flat tones of the section colour as full-width bands, or by 28px of space alone. A sixth row holds a one-word greeting at the same size followed, 24px to its right and centred on the x-height, by a solid round button 0.9em across with an arrow, which opens the email or booking link. Street and phone rows are links (map, call). No heading and no form. Optionally, one small round photograph sits at the right end of the first row.
On a phone: Rows at 9.6vw with 16px insets, about 84px tall; the hours break onto two rows (days, then times). The greeting row becomes a full-width band in the accent colour with the round arrow button at its right end, 56px across.

### 7. faq — Large questions, answers beside (table)
Every answer is open; nothing collapses. Each question-and-answer pair is a row on the 12-column grid: the question in columns 1–6 at clamp(26px, 2.6vw, 40px), medium weight, leading 1.1, no more than two lines; its answer in columns 8–12 at 17–18px with leading 1.55 and a measure near 40ch, its first baseline level with the question's first baseline. Rows are separated by 72–88px of empty space and nothing else. Five to seven rows. The section heading, two or three words, is set at the same size as the questions but in the softer ink and sits as the first row's left cell with an empty right cell, so it reads as the top of the same table rather than a title above it. Because questions are large and answers small, the left column works as a scannable index.
On a phone: Each pair stacks: question at 24px, answer 12px beneath at 17px, then 56px of space before the next. The heading leads at the same 24px in the softer ink. Answers stay open; there is no accordion on the phone either.

### 8. footer — Name first, places under it (list)
The wordmark opens the footer instead of closing it. On a full-bleed field in the page's inverse or final chapter colour, the name spans the full content width as the first thing, its cap line pushed 8% above the footer's top edge so the tops of the letters are sliced off by the section (`overflow: hidden` and a negative top margin in `em`). 72px beneath, a two-part row: columns 1–5 list the places or ways to reach the studio (cities, or email and telephone), one per line at 32–36px in the accent ink, each a link; columns 7–12 hold three columns of small links at 15px, top-aligned with the first place. A final row 96px lower carries the legal line at the left and a back-to-top text link at the right, both 13px. The field is about 90svh tall, and nothing sits between the wordmark and the row but space.
On a phone: The sliced wordmark stays at the top, fitted to the width. Places follow as a list at 26px, then the link columns two across at 15px. Legal line and back-to-top link share the last row at 12px, at opposite margins.

Adjusted for this page:
- It sets text at 12px: use your small step (13px or more) instead.
- It crops letters along their top: the inspector fails that (jurors read it as broken). Crop at the bottom edge or a side instead.

## 8. Motion — Masked headline
On load, each line of the headline rises from behind its own baseline, about 900ms with a strong ease-out, the second line 110ms after the first; the supporting sentence and the action then fade up together. Nothing else on the page performs. Because headlines are two lines at most, you author the line breaks and wrap each line yourself. Recipe: 'Masked line reveal' in references/motion.md.
This is the signature move, the one loud moment. Around it the page still needs the rest of the motion layer (SKILL.md step 9): an entrance for the parts of every section, feedback on every control, and one moving picture. The inspector fails a page that barely moves. Recipes: references/motion.md.

## 10. Where the richness comes from
Scale contrast and colour mass. A capability list set at 5vw is an image in its own right.

## 11. How this direction goes wrong
Without pictures, weak copy has nowhere to hide: write real sentences. Vary the set pieces; four sections of big-heading-plus-paragraph is a text document, not a design. Keep all headings within two lines.

## Always
Headlines two lines or fewer at every width. No eyebrow labels, no monospace, no 01/02/03, no thin rules, no gradients, no shadows at rest,
no cream-and-terracotta, no violet. The frame: one h1 on the first screen that says what this is, equal outer margins, no
section huddled on one side with the other half empty, no blank stretch over half a screen, a heading on every section.
`inspect.mjs` measures all of it.
